## Design principles

1. Define the source of truth before automating.
2. Validate before writing.
3. Make duplicate prevention and idempotency explicit.
4. Separate happy-path automation from exception handling.
5. Use human approval where business risk is meaningful.
6. Add monitoring, retry logic, and post-write verification.
7. Choose n8n, Make, Zapier, GoHighLevel, ClickUp, Apps Script, or local AI based on the process rather than tool loyalty.
