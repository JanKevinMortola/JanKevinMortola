## How I think about automation

I start with the operating process before choosing the platform. I identify the source of truth, required relationships, failure points, exception states, approvals, and measurable outputs first. Then I choose the right combination of n8n, Make, Zapier, GoHighLevel, ClickUp, Google Workspace, APIs, and AI based on maintainability, cost, complexity, risk, and the people who will operate the system.
