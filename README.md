I design practical automation and CRM systems that connect business operations, sales, customer data, AI, and the tools teams already use. My focus is reducing repetitive work, improving operational visibility, and turning fragmented workflows into dependable systems.

- **Make** — multi-step scenarios, data transformation, SaaS integrations, and visual workflow orchestration
- **Zapier** — fast SaaS-to-SaaS automation, triggers, actions, lead routing, and business app integrations
- **GoHighLevel (GHL)** — CRM workflows, lead pipelines, follow-up automation, contact management, and sales-to-operations handoffs
- **ClickUp** — operations systems, connected records, relationships, tracking structures, and automation-ready workspaces
- **Google Apps Script & Workspace** — lightweight internal tools, forms, Sheets-based systems, and integrations
- **APIs & Webhooks** — custom integrations across SaaS tools and internal processes
- **Agentic AI** — controlled AI workflows, tool use, human-in-the-loop processes, and governed automation
- **Self-hosted / local AI** — local model experimentation and AI workflows where privacy, cost, or infrastructure control matter

## How I think about automation

I start with the operating process before choosing the platform. I identify the source of truth, required relationships, failure points, exception states, approvals, and measurable outputs first. Then I choose the right combination of n8n, Make, Zapier, GoHighLevel, ClickUp, Google Workspace, APIs, and AI based on maintainability, cost, complexity, and the people who will operate the system.
