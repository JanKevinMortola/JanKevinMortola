# Jan Kevin Mortola

**AI Automation Specialist & Operations Systems Consultant**

I design practical automation systems that connect business operations, data, AI, and the tools teams already use. My focus is reducing repetitive work, improving operational visibility, and turning fragmented workflows into dependable systems.

## What I work on

- **n8n automation** — workflow design, debugging, APIs, webhooks, JSON mapping, branching, retries, and production hardening
- **Operations systems** — connected records, exceptions, reporting, approvals, reminders, and process architecture
- **ClickUp systems** — operational databases, relationships, tracking structures, and automation-ready workspaces
- **Google Apps Script & Workspace** — lightweight internal tools, forms, Sheets-based systems, and integrations
- **Agentic AI** — controlled AI workflows, tool use, human-in-the-loop processes, and governed automation
- **Self-hosted / local AI** — local model experimentation and AI workflows where privacy, cost, or infrastructure control matter
- **API integrations** — connecting SaaS tools and internal processes through REST APIs and webhooks

## How I think about automation

I start with the operating process before choosing the automation tool. I identify the source of truth, required relationships, failure points, exception states, approvals, and measurable outputs first. The goal is not simply to connect apps—it is to build a workflow people can trust and operate.
